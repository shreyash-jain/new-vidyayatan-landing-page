The expensive mistakes do not look like mistakes for four months. Demos land, the deck stays green — then integration starts, the engineers you interviewed were never assigned, and nothing says who owns the repository.

Choosing a **software development company in India** is a risk-allocation exercise wearing a shortlist's clothes. Cost overrun, slipped dates and ambiguously owned IP are visible before you sign. We are one of those vendors; these are the checks we would want run on us.

## Why India Remains a Top Destination for Outsourced Software Development

India's technology sector should cross [$315 billion in FY26, employing roughly 6 million people](https://nasscom.in/knowledge-center/publications/technology-sector-india-strategic-review-2026) — about $52,000 of revenue per head. IT outsourcing India is no bargain bin, and a quote far below that tells you who will write your code.

The depth cuts both ways. The country also hosts [2,117 global capability centres](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/) recruiting from your offshore development team's pool. Six million engineers means the gap between the best supplier and the worst is enormous — and both have the same website.

## How to Evaluate a Software Development Company in India: Portfolio, Domain Expertise & Certifications

**Portfolios are curated. Ask about the parts that were not.** Ask what broke in production: a team that has run a system answers in seconds, one that shipped and left describes features again. Ask to see code, too — [what looks finished but is not](/blog/the-hidden-cost-of-ai-generated-software) surfaces in month six.

**Domain expertise is a claim about incidents, not industries.** "We've worked in fintech" is a category. "We've handled a reconciliation break during a settlement window" is experience.

**Certifications certify a management system, not your project.** ISO/IEC 27001 covers [an information security management system within a declared scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001), so read the scope statement — one covering a single delivery centre says nothing about your team — and verify the body on [IAF CertSearch](https://www.iafcertsearch.org/).

![What an ISO 27001 certificate covers versus what your project needs: the declared ISMS scope names specific units and locations, while your work sits with a named team, on your repositories, under your data-handling rules — and the gap between the two is what the scope statement makes visible](/images/blog/software-development-company-india-certification-scope.svg)

## Engagement Models to Consider Before You Hire a Software Development Company

| | Fixed price | Dedicated development team | Staff augmentation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best when** | Scope is settled | Roadmap moving | Leadership in place, capacity short |
| **Scope risk sits with** | The vendor | You | You |
| **Typical failure** | Fighting over "done" | Drift, no owner | Skilled people, no architect |

Fixed price transfers risk to the vendor, and the vendor prices that transfer. A dedicated development team costs less per change, but only if someone on your side owns the roadmap — full breakdown in [choosing an engagement model](/blog/choosing-an-engagement-model).

## Time Zone, Communication, and IP-Protection Concerns Addressed

India runs on UTC+5:30 and **does not observe daylight saving**, so the overlap window moves twice a year on your side, not theirs.

![Time zone overlap between an India working day and four client markets: London gets four to five hours of overlap, Berlin five to six, and both New York and San Francisco get none unless the India team shifts its hours](/images/blog/software-development-company-india-timezone-overlap.svg)

London and Berlin get half a working day free. New York and San Francisco get none: US overlap must be bought with an India-side shift to 23:00 IST — a contract commitment, not a sales-call assurance.

On IP, one clause matters more than the NDA. Under India's Copyright Act an assignment must specify duration and territory: if the period is silent [it is deemed five years, and if the territory is silent it is presumed to extend within India only](https://copyright.gov.in/documents/copyrightrules1957.pdf). Boilerplate with neither leaves you five years of Indian rights to software you paid to own.

India has [no EU adequacy decision](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/adequacy-decisions_en), so EU transfers need Standard Contractual Clauses plus the processor terms [GDPR Article 28](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-28-gdpr/) requires. For Indian personal data, [the DPDP Act applies too](/blog/dpdp-act-schools-edtech-compliance).

## A Due-Diligence Checklist Before You Hire a Software Development Company in India

Run this software outsourcing checklist on every shortlisted custom software development partner.

1. Two references **you** chose, including one project that went badly.
2. Named engineers, with CVs and notice before anyone is swapped.
3. The ISO certificate itself — scope read, body verified.
4. The IP assignment clause, stating duration and territory.
5. A data processing agreement, with SCCs where EU/UK data leaves.
6. A paid pilot: one real slice of work, four to six weeks.
7. Overlap hours as a commitment, naming who works the late shift.
8. The exit clause — notice, handover, transition cost — and who holds root credentials.

## Common questions

**Is charging for a pilot a red flag?**
The opposite. Free work is either a subsidised sales process or staffed with whoever is spare. Four to six weeks of paid work is the cheapest diligence available.

**Is India cheaper than nearshore options?**
Usually on rate, not on total. Nearshore vs offshore development buys overlap hours at a higher rate — the comparison settles once you price the meetings you need.

**What if they won't give references we choose?**
Treat the refusal as the answer. A confident supplier lets you pick from the client list, including a project that went badly.

**Do we need a lawyer for the IP clause?**
For anything you intend to own, yes. Those defaults are easy to miss, and an hour of counsel beats a disputed assignment.

**When is a company too small for all this?**
Below roughly $30,000 of work most of it is disproportionate — run a pilot, keep the contract short, hold the credentials. Same judgement as whether to [build at all](/blog/habuild-build-vs-buy).
