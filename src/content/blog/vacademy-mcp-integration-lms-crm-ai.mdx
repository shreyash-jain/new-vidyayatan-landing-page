Every institute has the same Monday-morning question: "How many new enquiries did we get, and who hasn't been called back?" The answer is in your CRM. Getting it out means logging in, picking filters, exporting a sheet and reading it.

Now you can just ask. **Vacademy's MCP integration connects your LMS and CRM to AI tools**, so the assistant you already use can read your institute's data and help with the task, instead of you copying numbers into a chat window.

## What is MCP, in plain words?

MCP stands for **Model Context Protocol**. It is an open standard, introduced by Anthropic in 2024, that lets an AI tool connect to another piece of software in a controlled way. Think of it as a standard plug. Without it, every AI tool needs its own custom connection to every system. With it, one connection works for any AI tool that supports MCP.

For your institute, that means the AI no longer works from guesses or pasted text. It asks Vacademy directly and gets your real records.

## What can your team ask it to do?

The connection covers both sides of the platform: the **LMS** (courses and learners) and the **CRM** (leads, calls and follow-ups).

![Everyday tasks by team: owners ask for a summary of enquiries, leads and results; admissions teams ask who to call back and for meeting and follow-up status; educators ask about course and learner progress; operations teams ask for reports and website drafts](/images/blog/vacademy-mcp-everyday-tasks.svg)

- **Institute owners** can ask for a quick picture: new enquiries this week, where leads are stuck, how a campaign is doing.
- **Admissions and counselling teams** can ask which leads need a follow-up, what was said on the last call and which meetings are coming up.
- **Educators** can look up courses and how learners are progressing without opening several screens.
- **Operations teams** can pull reports, review messages and get website pages drafted.

The pattern is the same each time. You describe the job in your own words, and the AI does the clicking and collating.

## Is it safe? Who can see what?

This is the first question every institute should ask, so here are the plain answers.

- **It is scoped to you.** The connection is limited to your institute and to the staff member who approved it. It can't wander into another institute's data.
- **Changes are drafts.** When the AI edits something, such as a website page, it is saved as a draft. Nothing goes live until a person reviews it and publishes.
- **A person stays in charge.** The AI works with the access of the person who connected it, no more.

None of that removes the need for judgement. AI tools can misread a request or summarise a report badly, so check anything important before acting on it, the same as you would with a new team member's first week of work.

## Why connect your LMS and CRM to AI at all?

Because most of the time spent on institute software is not decisions. It is searching, filtering, copying and re-typing. That is the part an AI tool does well, and it is the part your staff least enjoy.

It also puts the CRM and LMS in one conversation. A question like "which enquiries were for courses that have seats left?" touches both, and normally means two screens and a spreadsheet.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is MCP integration for Vacademy?

It is a connection that lets MCP-compatible AI tools read and work with your Vacademy LMS and CRM data, so you can ask for what you need instead of navigating screens.

### Do I need technical skills to use it?

No. You ask in everyday language. A technical person may be needed once to approve the connection.

### Can the AI change things without my approval?

Edits such as website changes are saved as drafts. A person reviews and publishes them.

### Is my institute's data shared with other institutes?

No. The connection is scoped to your own institute and to the person who authorised it.

### Does it cover both the LMS and the CRM?

Yes. It spans learners and courses on the LMS side, and leads, calls, meetings and reports on the CRM side.
