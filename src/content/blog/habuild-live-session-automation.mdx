As a fitness community grows, manual session management becomes an impossible
bottleneck. HABUILD eliminated it entirely:

- **20 lakh+ users** across 36 nations
- **10x growth** in 10 months
- **100%** automated attendance
- **Zero** manual link creation

## The live session nightmare

- **Manual link creation** — creating and sending unique Zoom/Meet links to
  thousands of participants, for every single session.
- **Attendance tracking chaos** — manually cross-referencing who attended what
  across multiple platforms.
- **Administrative overload** — more time on logistics than on actually coaching
  members.

## The automated solution

- **Unique links for every user** — personalised session links across Zoom,
  YouTube and Google Meet for secure, trackable access. Zero manual work.
- **Automated attendance tracking** — participation marked automatically, no
  spreadsheets. 100% accurate.
- **Recorded sessions as live** — pre-recorded content can be presented as live
  sessions for flexible scheduling and consistency.
- **Multi-platform integration** — instructors keep the tools they prefer; the
  system doesn't care which platform streams the class.

## How it works

1. **Session planning** — instructors schedule through the dashboard: platform,
   time, member segments.
2. **Automatic link generation** — unique, secure links generated per registered
   member.
3. **Smart distribution** — personalised notifications via WhatsApp, email or
   app, each with the member's own access link.
4. **Automated tracking** — real-time attendance, engagement and post-session
   analytics with no manual intervention.

> "The session automation completely changed our operations. We went from
> spending hours on logistics to focusing entirely on member experience."
> — HABUILD Operations Team

## Under the hood

- **Smart session routing** — an algorithm picks the optimal platform and
  settings per member, device and session type.
- **Cross-platform analytics** — one dashboard aggregating data from every
  streaming platform.
- **Real-time scaling** — infrastructure that adjusts automatically to any
  number of concurrent sessions.
