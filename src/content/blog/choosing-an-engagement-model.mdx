One of the first decisions when starting a software project isn't technical — it's
how you'll work with your development partner. Get it right and delivery feels
effortless. Get it wrong and you'll spend more time managing the relationship
than shipping product.

We offer three engagement models, and each fits a different situation.

## 1. Project Outsourcing

**Best when the scope is well-defined.** You know what you want built, you want a
fixed set of milestones, and you want full ownership handed over on completion.

This is the lowest-overhead option for you: we take responsibility for delivery
end to end, transfer all deliverables and documentation, and you're free to run
with it afterwards.

## 2. Dedicated Team

**Best when the roadmap is still evolving.** You get hand-picked engineers who
work exclusively under your direction, integrate with your standups, and can scale
up or down as priorities shift.

Think of it as extending your in-house team without the hiring overhead — ideal
when you need velocity and flexibility more than a fixed scope.

## 3. Technology Partnership

**Best for long-term growth.** We dive into your business, co-own the roadmap,
and continuously optimise your architecture and infrastructure. You get a
dedicated point of contact and a partner genuinely invested in your outcomes.

## A simple way to choose

| Your situation | Best model |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed, well-understood scope | Project Outsourcing |
| Evolving product, need velocity | Dedicated Team |
| Long-term, strategic build | Technology Partnership |

Still unsure? You can also **start small** — a focused discovery or first
milestone — and move between models as your needs change.
