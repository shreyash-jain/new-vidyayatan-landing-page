In a mobile-first world, email is often ignored. HABUILD cracked the code of
user retention by building an intelligent WhatsApp automation engine that still
feels personal.

## Mobile-first engagement

HABUILD realised early that users weren't checking email for workout reminders —
they were on WhatsApp. But manually messaging 500 people is hard. Messaging
20 lakh people is impossible.

The challenge:

- Timely reminders before 6 AM classes
- Consistency tracking (streaks)
- Personalised motivation based on activity

A sample morning sequence:

> **5:45 AM** — "Good morning, Sarah! ☀️ Ready for day 15 of your streak?"
>
> **5:45 AM** — "Here is your unique link for today's Yoga session:
> habuild.in/live/xyz"
>
> **5:46 AM** — "Joined! See you on the mat. 🧘‍♀️"

## The logic behind the messages

It's not a broadcast — it's a logic-driven conversation engine.

- **Pre-session triggers.** Fifteen minutes before class, the system queries the
  active member database, generates unique tracking links, and dispatches
  messages via the WhatsApp Business API.
- **Attendance confirmation.** Post-session, the system checks who attended.
  Attendees get a "Great job!" that boosts their streak count.
- **Re-engagement loops.** Missed 3 days? A gentle "We miss you" flow triggers
  automatically — often with a quick 10-minute catch-up video.

## The results

- **98%** open rate on WhatsApp
- **40%** increase in retention
- **0** manual messages sent
- **24/7** automated member support
