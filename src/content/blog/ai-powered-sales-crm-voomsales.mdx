## Why businesses need an AI-powered sales CRM

Salespeople spend most of their day not selling. In Salesforce's [sixth State of Sales report](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/sales-ai-statistics-2024/), 5,500 sales professionals said **70% of their time goes on non-selling tasks** like admin and meeting prep.

![Salesforce's State of Sales survey of 5,500 sales professionals found reps spend 70% of their time on non-selling tasks and 30% selling; an AI sales CRM takes on typing in leads, remembering follow-ups, making first calls and building reports](/images/blog/ai-sales-crm-time-split.svg)

A traditional CRM doesn't fix that. It stores data, but someone still has to type it in, remember the follow-up and build the report. Follow-ups slip, and the manager sees the pipeline only when someone updates it.

AI takes the repetitive part: ranking leads, making the first call, sending reminders, pulling the numbers together. The rep still closes the deal.

## Key features of VoomSales

### Lead management

Every lead lands in one list, with its source, status, owner and last contact. Each gets a score from 0 to 100, based on source quality, completeness, recency and engagement, and is tagged Hot, Warm or Cold.

That is **AI lead management** in practice. A rep starts the day with the hottest leads at the top, not just the newest.

### Sales pipeline management

A pipeline is the path from first enquiry to signed deal.

![A six-stage sales pipeline and what the CRM does at each: new lead scored 0 to 100 and sent to a rep on shift; contacted with an AI call in minutes and WhatsApp if no answer; qualified with answers saved on the record; proposal, where a timer shows if the rep has gone quiet; negotiation, where stalled deals stand out; won or lost, feeding the conversion reports](/images/blog/ai-sales-crm-pipeline.svg)

Laid out like this, it shows where deals stall. Twenty deals stuck at Proposal for three weeks is Monday's conversation.

### Sales automation

VoomSales's workflow engine has 20 building blocks. Conditions, delays, loops and "place an AI call" are among them. A typical rule: if the lead doesn't pick up, try again in two hours, then send a WhatsApp message. This is where **sales automation software** earns back real hours.

### Customer relationship management

Calls, WhatsApp chats and notes sit on the lead's record, so a rep sees the whole history before saying hello. Calls are recorded, transcribed in Hindi and English, and scored against the team's own checklist.

### Sales reporting and visibility

There are 15 report views, from lead performance and pipeline status to calling activity and each rep's conversions. Two timers run on every lead: time to first contact, and time since the rep last acted. A manager sees who is falling behind while the lead can still be saved.

## How an AI sales CRM works

## How to choose an AI-powered sales CRM

Before you pick one, check that it:

- captures leads from where yours actually come from
- has a pipeline you can shape to your own stages
- automates follow-ups, not just reminders
- shows which AI features are live today, not "coming soon"
- reports on what your manager checks every week
- connects to your phone system, WhatsApp and ad accounts
- is easy enough that reps will actually use it
- keeps customer data secure and lets you export it
